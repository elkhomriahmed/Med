# Shot list: Why Lighthouse Keepers Went Mad (script v3)

One picture per clause. World: lamp room on top of the tower, tower on a rock, sea around it, harbour town across the water.
Camera flies between places; hard cut only for real time jumps (marked CUT), dissolves for night ↔ morning.
