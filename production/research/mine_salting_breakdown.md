# Breakdown: "How Mine Salting Worked" (61.1 s, 720x1280, the competitor's latest short)

## Story and pictures
- Hook: shotgun loaded with gold under a single ceiling lamp (volumetric beam + dust). Then: "fired it into the walls of a mine",
  the buddy eating a sandwich who "watches the gold vanish into the rock", "salting" explained, the mine on a cliff, the rails,
  the newspaper gag ("THE HOPEFUL HERALD – MILLIONAIRE DUE TO-MORROW"), the bank counter (bills → plate of gold dust), the rich man's
  car, "SURE THING MINE EST. 1895", the expert with a pan and magnifying glass, "completely insane" reaction, dynamite twist,
  explosion, the real vein, "10 FT" graphic.
- About 10 places, all in one connected world (mine tunnel, wooden shaft room, cliff, rails, western street, bank, desert road,
  mine entrance). Every line has its own picture; macro inserts for objects (gold dust plate, pan swirl, fuse).
- Text gags: newspaper headline, calendar "SEPTEMBER 1909", crate "CANDLES 40 LBS", mine sign, on-screen "10 FT" arrow.

## Characters
- Heads: warm cream (not white), lit by the scene (amber in the mine), slightly darker toward the outline, so they read as spheres.
  Hair shows under the hats; one has an ear.
- Outline: thick, warm dark brown, the same on head, body and hat.
- Torsos: bean/pear shapes about as wide as the head, clearly clothes: plaid shirt with suspenders, knit sweater with collar and
  buttons, white double-breasted suit with buttons and a red rose, purple coat. Hats are real 3D objects (ranger hat with lamp,
  knit beanie, top hat, bowler) that sit ON the head.
- Limbs: thin dark lines, small dark mitten hands, small oval feet. Hands hold props (shotgun, sandwich, newspaper, dynamite, pan).
- Expressions: calm most of the time (half-lidded, small mouth, wink); big ones are short beats (laugh, teeth grimace).

## World and light
- Every surface has relief: painted rock with cracks and bump, wood planks with grain and dark gaps, gravel and pebbles,
  rails with ties. Ceilings are built (rock, beams).
- Practical light sources in shot (ceiling lamp, lanterns, candles) light the characters; strong soft cast shadows on the floor;
  dark corners; volumetric beam with dust. Exteriors: warm sunlit rock against a cool blue sky.
- Grade: warm amber dominant, rich saturation, highlights never clipped on faces.

## Camera and framing
- Mostly medium and wide shots with full bodies; heads ≈ 20–27% of frame width; close-ups only for jokes.
- Slow pushes and pans, quick moves between places, one white dust-out at the explosion (story beat, not a cut flash).

## What it means for us (all in STYLE_BIBLE v12 rules)
- Real light rendering (R1), cream lit spherical faces (C1–C2), bean torsos with real clothes (C4–C5), mitten hands (C6),
  framing at ~21% head width (F1), built ceilings and relief on every surface (W1–W2), practical lights in shot (L1–L2).
