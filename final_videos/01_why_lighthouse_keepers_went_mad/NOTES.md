## What worked (keep doing it)
- One connected world (lamp room on the tower, reef, jetty, sea, harbour town) with fast camera flights between places.
- Every line with its own picture + small gags (sweat drop, "?", music notes, sandwich bite, binocular gulls, "MARY ANN" board).
- Sound effects pinned to their words; steady music bed; −14 LUFS master; captions one word, bouncing on the spoken word.
- Characters: no limb/hand/outline pops after the characters-agent fixes; hats and hair never cover the eyes.

## What went wrong (NOT fixed in this video — never repeat in the next ones)
1. **Camera transitions lacking in some scenes** (some moves feel abrupt or empty).
2. **Sound effects not 100 % accurate** to what's on screen.
3. **Quoted character lines keep the narrator's tone**: the keeper's "Son, I've turned that thing…" must sound like the old keeper
   (different voice/tone for character quotes).
4. **Some voiceover parts sound exaggerated**: generate calmer (higher stability, less style) and check every line by ear-proxy
   (pitch/energy outliers) before building.
5. **Colour palettes not in harmony** (yellow coat + orange room + brown floor + gold lens all fight): pick one palette per place
   (one dominant + one accent family, characters contrasting with it) before building.
6. **Context changes without a flight (the hard cuts at "You see," 3.1 s and "By winter" 42.4 s) looked glitchy** → people scroll
   away. Every non-flight transition must be a designed, deliberate transition, never a raw cut.
7. **Backgrounds not 3D or textured enough**, especially the town: flat facades, little relief and depth (see STYLE_BIBLE note
   2026-11-17 "3D depth and texture").
