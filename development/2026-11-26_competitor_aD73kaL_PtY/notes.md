# Why his road world reads as real 3D and ours reads flat (2026-11-26)
1. Trees: real branching trunks (bark grooves) + thousands of individual leaves in clumps; light passes through; dappled shadows. Ours: sphere blobs.
2. Clouds: painterly soft cumulus (flat bottoms, wispy edges, sunlit tops, blue-grey undersides). Ours: lumpy sphere clusters.
3. Light: very bright sunny high-key exposure, warm sun, strong bounce, bloom around the sun/glints, long soft shadows. Ours: dull grey.
4. Colour: saturated lime greens, golden/green furrowed fields, clean blue sky. Ours: grey-green.
5. Ground: lush grass tufts with light tips, furrowed crop rows to the horizon (3D-looking stripes). Ours: flat texture.
6. Road: smooth blue-grey asphalt, subtle cracks, crisp markings. Ours: noisy white speckles.
7. Camera: low angles + lens blur, big foreground objects (tree, sign) for depth.

## Colour, shades, saturation, warmth (measured on 8 daylight frames vs our countryside frame)
| | His daylight frames | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| mean brightness | 0.52–0.79 (avg ~0.63) | 0.49 |
| bright pixels (>85%) | 9–59% (typ ~20%) | 1% |
| dark pixels (<25%) | 2–16% | 23% |
| mean saturation | ~0.26–0.36 | 0.28 |
| warm saturated pixels (golden fields, warm light, wood) | 10–42% | 5% |
- High-key sunny look: lots of near-white sky/clouds/sunlit surfaces, shadows lifted (soft blue, never black), very few deep darks in day.
- Warmth from the sun + golden/yellow fields + warm wood; greens are lime/yellow-green in the sun, deeper green only in shade.
- Saturation is moderate overall but clean (no grey/mud): vivid accents (red neon sign, police green, orange hard hat).
- Night/storm frames are the only dark, desaturated ones (story weather).
